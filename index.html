Who we are

Our Capture is a visual solutions company headquartered in Thohoyandou, Limpopo. Since 2019 we have supported businesses, event organisers, institutions and public-sector clients with professional photography, videography and event coverage.

We pair creative craft with a disciplined production process — clear scoping, planned logistics, briefed crews, secure file handling and predictable delivery — so the organisations we work with always know what to expect.

Where a project also calls for digital or technology work, we deliver it together with our partner Tech Dev Solutions, giving clients a single point of contact across both creative and technical scope.

Engagement Model

How we work with you

A predictable process with clear ownership at every stage.

Discovery & brief

We meet to understand the objective, audience, deliverables, dates and any brand or access requirements.

Proposal & scope

A written proposal covering scope, crew, deliverables, timelines and a fixed quotation — no surprises.

Planning & logistics

Shot lists, run sheets, site visits, permissions and equipment prep agreed before the shoot day.

Production

Professional-grade crew and equipment on site, working to the agreed plan with on-shoot data backup.

Edit, review & delivery

Edited selects for your review, one round of refinements, then final files via secure transfer in web and print-ready formats.

What you can expect

Standards we hold ourselves to on every engagement.

  • A single, named point of contact
  • Agreed turnaround times, confirmed in writing
  • Deliverables in web and print-ready formats
  • Secure file transfer and redundant backups
  • Confidentiality agreements accommodated on request
  • Insured, professional-grade equipment with on-site redundancy
  • Fixed quotations and compliant tax invoicing
